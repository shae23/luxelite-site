LuxeLite Rentals

Chairs, tables, linens, delivery and events in St. Ann, Jamaica

LuxeLite Rentals is the Android app we use to run our event rental business. It is a private business tool for the owners and their staff. It is not a public service.

What the app does

  • Keeps bookings, rental dates, delivery zones and multi-day pricing in one place.
  • Tracks stock, so we know what is free on a given date.
  • Records customer details, payments received and business expenses.
  • Imports from and exports to Excel.
  • Syncs the business data between our phones through a file in our own Google Drive.

How it uses Google

The app asks to sign in with a Google account so it can keep one backup file, named LuxeLite_Data.json, in that account's Google Drive. It can only see files that the app itself created. It cannot see anything else in the Drive. The data file is used to back up the business data and to keep our phones in step with each other.
