Privacy Policy

LuxeLite Rentals. Last updated: October 8, 2026.

Who we are

LuxeLite Rentals is an event rental business in St. Ann, Jamaica. This policy covers the LuxeLite Rentals app, which we use to run our business. Contact: luxeliterentalsja@gmail.com.

Information we keep

We keep the records needed to run the business: customer contact details, bookings, payments and expenses. We enter this information ourselves.

Google account and Google Drive

The app is for our own use. When we sign in with Google, the app asks for access only to the files it creates itself in Google Drive. It uses that access to keep a backup copy of our business records and to keep our devices up to date. It cannot see or change any other file in the Drive. It also shows which Google account is signed in.

We do not use this information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone outside the business.

LuxeLite Rentals' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see the information

Only the people who run the business.

Deleting information

To ask us to correct or delete your details, email us at the address above. You can also remove the app's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
