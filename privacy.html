Privacy Policy

LuxeLite Rentals app. Last updated: October 8, 2026.

Who we are

LuxeLite Rentals is an event rental business in St. Ann, Jamaica, owned by Tahnee Chung-Cypser and Shae Cypser. This policy covers the LuxeLite Rentals Android app, which we use to run the business. You can reach us at luxeliterentalsja@gmail.com.

What information the app holds

The app holds our business records: customer names, phone numbers, email addresses and addresses, bookings, rental dates, items rented, payments received, expenses, prices and delivery zones. We enter this information ourselves, or import it from our own Excel workbook.

Where it is stored

The records are stored in the app's private storage on the phone. If sync is switched on, the same records are also stored in one file named LuxeLite_Data.json in the Google Drive of the Google account that signed in. We do not run any server of our own, and the app does not send this information anywhere else.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for one permission: access to files in Google Drive that the app itself creates (the drive.file permission). With it, the app can create, read and update the LuxeLite_Data.json file. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

The app also reads the email address of the signed-in Google account, only to show on the Sync screen which account is connected. It does not store that address anywhere except on your phone.

We use this access only to back up the business records and to keep the business's phones in step. We do not use it for advertising. We do not sell it, share it with anyone, or let people read it, other than the people who sign in to the app with that Google account.

LuxeLite Rentals' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see the information

Only the people who use the app and who sign in to the shared Google account. Anyone with access to that Google account or to an unlocked phone with the app installed can see the records, so keep both secure.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Signing out in the app stops syncing. The records stay on the phone.
  • You can delete LuxeLite_Data.json from Google Drive at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the records from the phone.
  • You can withdraw the app's access to Google Drive at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To ask us to correct or delete a customer's record, email us at the address above.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not meant for children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
